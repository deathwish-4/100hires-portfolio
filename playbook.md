# Playbook: AI-Powered SEO Content Production for B2B SaaS

## Core Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### Phase 1: Topic Strategy & Hyper-Personalization

Do not build content around fictional average personas or generic queries.

- Abandon generic keyword-driven content like "best XYZ software" because AI search is moving toward real personalization that matches the actual user, not just the keywords (source: Eli Schwartz, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/schwartze_everyone-at-cannes-wanted-to-talk-about-ai-activity-7476254190856757248-iW0R?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/schwartze_everyone-at-cannes-wanted-to-talk-about-ai-activity-7476254190856757248-iW0R?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 26.06.2026)
- Prepare for the reality that AI bots will scrape standard informational content and serve answers directly in the SERP, which can lead to zero visitors and zero clicks for your domain (source: Bernard Huang, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bernardjhuang_ai-is-using-our-work-then-sending-us-none-activity-7474857489268711424-rQ-J?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bernardjhuang_ai-is-using-our-work-then-sending-us-none-activity-7474857489268711424-rQ-J?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 22.06.2026)



### Phase 2: Research & Achieving Information Gain

Do not use standard AI workflows that simply scrape and rewrite existing search results.

- Avoid basic AI article generators because they only rewrite the top 10 Google results, resulting in grammatically perfect but completely useless content with zero information gain (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)
- Deploy AI sub-agents to act as picky readers who identify what existing articles fail to answer, and use research agents to hunt for operator-level insights in places like Reddit threads, YouTube tutorials, and Twitter discussions (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)
- Once the deep research and information extraction are complete, use Claude to handle the actual writing process section by section (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)



### Phase 3: Drafting & Ensuring Genuine E-E-A-T

Your content must rely on true expertise to avoid algorithmic penalties.

- Do not publish biased, self-promoting pages at scale that pretend to be educational, as this is a major liability that leads to algorithmic demotion by Google (source: Lily Ray, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 22.07.2026)
- Do not simply ask a language model to "add information gain." Instead, build your content strictly on original research, genuine opinions, and true expertise (source: Lily Ray, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 22.07.2026)



### Phase 4: Technical On-Page Restraint

Avoid using AI for basic technical SEO optimizations, as it introduces severe risks.

- Do not use AI to exact-match meta titles on top-performing pages, as this is a high-risk decision that doesn't reflect how search engines currently rewrite titles (source: Mark Williams-Cook, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 23.07.2026)
- Avoid using AI to arbitrarily add internal links, mass-generate FAQ blocks (which creates "commodity content"), or refresh stats and publish dates, as this introduces a wildly high risk of hallucinations and fabricated data (source: Mark Williams-Cook, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 23.07.2026)



### Phase 5: Off-Page AI Corroboration

AI search engines rely heavily on third-party signals to validate your content.

- Treat AI search optimization primarily as a 3rd-party citation problem, acknowledging that for the SaaS vertical, 82.3% of top-cited AI sources come from outside the brand's controlled domain (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_ai-search-is-a-3rd-party-citation-problem-activity-7489666595074752513-bPGN?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_ai-search-is-a-3rd-party-citation-problem-activity-7489666595074752513-bPGN?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 02.08.2026)
- Combine on-page SEO with digital PR and social media (which is the primary driver for SaaS) to create a winning AI corroboration loop (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_ai-search-is-a-3rd-party-citation-problem-activity-7489666595074752513-bPGN?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_ai-search-is-a-3rd-party-citation-problem-activity-7489666595074752513-bPGN?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 02.08.2026)

## Where Experts Disagree

**Conflict #1: How to Source "Information Gain"**

- **What Author A (Gael Breton) recommends:** He advocates for using AI research agents to scrape human-led forums (like Reddit and Twitter) to extract insights and specific strategies that aren't found in the top 10 Google results (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)
- **What Author B (Lily Ray) recommends:** She argues that true uniqueness must come from original research, genuine opinions, and true expertise, warning heavily against asking language models to artificially inject information gain (source: Lily Ray, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_one-of-the-biggest-mistakes-i-see-site-owners-activity-7485421476964265984-g0A6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 22.07.2026)
- **Which side I take and why:** For enterprise B2B SaaS, I side with Lily Ray. While Gael's tactic of scraping Reddit is highly creative, enterprise buyers require verifiable expertise. Relying on anonymous forum data scraped by AI introduces significant factual risks for high-ticket SaaS products.

**Conflict #2: The Role of AI in the SEO Workflow**

- **What Author A (Gael Breton) recommends:** Embraces AI deeply in the workflow, using sub-agents to analyze content gaps, research agents to hunt for data, and Claude to handle the writing section by section (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)
- **What Author B (Mark Williams-Cook) recommends:** Strictly cautions against using AI for everyday SEO tasks, warning that using it to pull GSC reports, write FAQs, or update stats offers no real benefit and risks massive algorithmic traffic loss due to hallucinations (source: Mark Williams-Cook, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/markseo_seo-activity-7486065458039824385-s6ps?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 23.07.2026)
- **Which side I take and why:** I take a hybrid approach. Gael is correct that AI is powerful for synthesizing deep research, but Mark's warnings are crucial. AI should be restricted strictly to the drafting phase (as Gael suggests) and completely banned from making technical on-page modifications like meta titles or internal linking (as Mark warns).

**Conflict #3: The Threat vs. Utility of AI Bots and Scraping**

- **What Author A (Gael Breton) recommends:** Views AI bots as a massive utility. He deploys AI "research agents" to actively crawl and scrape external platforms (Reddit, YouTube, Twitter) to build his content (source: Gael Breton, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_your-ai-articles-read-fine-but-say-nothing-activity-7452295174195507200-4ks2?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 21.04.2026)
- **What Author B (Bernard Huang) recommends:** Views AI bots as an existential threat. He warns that AI bots are scraping publisher sites to answer questions directly in the SERP, resulting in over 80% bot traffic, zero clicks, and destroying the ROI of content creation (source: Bernard Huang, [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bernardjhuang_ai-is-using-our-work-then-sending-us-none-activity-7474857489268711424-rQ-J?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bernardjhuang_ai-is-using-our-work-then-sending-us-none-activity-7474857489268711424-rQ-J?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADSPYLcBeFksh9jq9nUKqCG40f5VmskTrOA) 22.06.2026)
- **Which side I take and why:** I side with Bernard Huang's threat assessment. While Gael uses bots to his advantage to build content, Bernard correctly identifies the end-state of the AI ecosystem: zero-click searches. For B2B SaaS, we must assume our informational content will be scraped by AI. Therefore, our strategy must pivot away from standard guides and toward proprietary tools or gated data that force the user to actually visit the site.

## What I Rejected and Why

1. **Idea Rejected: Automating technical on-page optimizations and content refreshes.**
  - **Reasoning:** I fully rejected the idea of using AI to add FAQs, update publish dates, or refresh statistics. As highlighted by Mark Williams-Cook, doing this at scale produces "commodity content" and introduces a wildly high risk of fabricated evidence that will destroy brand trust.
2. **Idea Rejected: Building marketing campaigns around average buyer personas.**
  - **Reasoning:** I rejected the traditional approach of writing for a fictional "VP of Marketing" persona. As Eli Schwartz pointed out, AI search is moving toward real personalization that shapes responses around the actual individual, meaning generic persona-driven content will stop converting.

## My Original Ideas

**Strategy: Utilizing Internal Support Tickets for "Zero-Click" Immunity**

None of the provided sources explicitly addressed how to counter the zero-click phenomenon using internal data. My original idea is to bypass standard keyword research and instead feed raw, anonymized customer support tickets and sales objections into the AI. By creating highly technical documentation that solves actual, complex workflows for existing customers, we create content that is too specific for an AI bot to easily summarize in a short SERP snippet, forcing the user to actually visit the site and bypassing the traffic loss described by Bernard Huang.

---



## Weaknesses of This Playbook

- **The Zero-Click Threat:** Even if we execute Phase 1 through 3 perfectly, Bernard Huang points out that AI bots can still scrape our deeply researched content and serve it directly to users, meaning we might still experience a massive drop in site visitors and conversions despite high content quality.
- **High Production Costs:** By rejecting automated AI drafting and insisting on genuine original research combined with extensive Digital PR efforts, the time and financial cost to produce and rank a single piece of content will be exceptionally high compared to traditional SEO methods.

## Who I Would NOT Recommend Following and Why

**Not Recommended for B2B SaaS: Gael Breton**

While Gael Breton's multi-agent workflow is innovative, I would not recommend his specific research strategy for an Enterprise B2B SaaS domain. He relies on instructing AI agents to scrape YouTube tutorials, Reddit threads, and Twitter discussions to find core advice. For B2B enterprise software, sourcing data from unverified, anonymous Reddit users is a major liability. Furthermore, as Mark Williams-Cook noted, relying on AI to gather and synthesize factual evidence carries a wildly high risk of fabrication, which could be disastrous for a SaaS brand's E-E-A-T.
